# 🎙️ Video Demonstration Script (3–5 Minutes)

**Project Title:** Electric Vehicle (EV) Industry Analytics & Intelligence Platform  
**Target Audience:** Evaluation Mentor / Academic Evaluator / Hiring Panel  
**Estimated Time:** 3 minutes 30 seconds  

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & Problem Statement
> *"Hello everyone! My name is [Your Name], and today I am excited to present my individual capstone data analytics project in the Transportation domain: the **Electric Vehicle (EV) Industry Analytics and Intelligence Platform**.*
>
> *The automotive market is transitioning toward zero-emission mobility. However, manufacturers, business analysts, and researchers face challenges in understanding battery adoption trends, geographical production hubs, price-to-range efficiencies, and real-world environmental impacts. In this project, I developed an end-to-end data pipeline, comprehensive exploratory analysis, and an interactive executive dashboard covering 1,200 vehicle models across 12 global automakers and 5 battery chemistries from 2021 to 2026."*

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Executive Overview & Technical Architecture
*(Show the browser on the `Executive Overview` tab)*
> *"Let's look at the Executive Overview. At a glance, our top KPI cards provide immediate market visibility: over 35 million EV deliveries tracked, generating nearly 1.5 trillion dollars in gross market revenue, with an average driving range of 406 km and a 4.21 out of 5 customer satisfaction score.*
>
> *The dashboard includes real-time multi-filtering. When I filter by year, say 2024, or select a manufacturer like Tesla or BYD, all charts and metrics adapt instantaneously.*
>
> *Our charts highlight two major trends: first, SUVs and two-wheelers are driving the highest delivery volumes; second, battery chemistries are shifting rapidly from traditional nickel-cobalt towards cost-efficient Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) and next-generation Solid-State cells."*

---

### [1:15 - 2:00] Persona Scenario 1: Emily Johnson (Market Strategy)
*(Click on tab `Scenario 1: Emily Johnson`)*
> *"Next, the platform addresses three real-world business scenarios. Scenario 1 is built for **Emily Johnson**, an EV Market Strategy Director.*
>
> *Emily needs to identify which vehicle segments yield the highest returns. Our dual-axis category analysis demonstrates that while two-wheelers deliver high volume in urban markets, electric SUVs generate over 45% of total revenue. Furthermore, our Price versus Rating scatter plot shows that customer satisfaction plateaus around the $45,000 to $55,000 price point, suggesting OEMs should target C-segment SUVs with balanced LFP battery configurations."*

---

### [2:00 - 2:40] Persona Scenario 2: David Chen (Business Intelligence)
*(Click on tab `Scenario 2: David Chen`)*
> *"Scenario 2 addresses **David Chen**, an Automotive Business Intelligence Manager advising clients entering the EV sector.*
>
> *David needs to evaluate competitor market shares and cost efficiency. Here, we visualize the battery technology mix across each major OEM. We can see how BYD and Tesla have achieved a distinct pricing advantage by deploying LFP packs. Our Price-to-Range Efficiency metric shows that manufacturers dual-sourcing cells maintain an average ratio of under $95 per kilometer of range, giving them a durable competitive moat."*
