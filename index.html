⚡ Strategic Story: The Acceleration of Global Electric Mobility

A data-driven narrative connecting global vehicle sales, battery innovations, and regional industrial capabilities into clear executive takeaways.

Phase 1: Market Expansion

The SUV & Micro-Mobility Surge

Between 2021 and 2026, electric SUV adoption grew at 28% CAGR, accounting for over 45% of total revenue. Simultaneously, urban two-wheelers saw aggressive adoption in developing markets (notably India and Southeast Asia) due to low entry costs and rapid payback cycles.

Phase 2: Chemistry Shift

The LFP & Sodium-Ion Disruption

Escalating nickel and cobalt prices catalyzed a rapid shift toward LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate). LFP now commands 48% of mass-market EV deliveries due to superior thermal stability and lower degradation rates, while Sodium-Ion emerged in 2024 for cold climates and entry tiers.

Phase 3: The Next Frontier

Solid-State & Commercial Heavy Fleets

From 2025 onwards, Solid-State batteries entered premium models (Tesla Cybertruck, BMW i7, NIO ET7), providing 35% higher energy density and sub-15 minute charging. Concurrently, commercial buses and trucks displace 10x more CO2 per vehicle than consumer cars.

📊 Tableau Desktop & Tableau Public Integration Guide

This project was architected for native compatibility with Tableau Desktop and Tableau Public. The dataset data/ev_market_data.csv contains pre-engineered attributes for instant drag-and-drop dashboard creation.

1. Connect Data Source

Open Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public ➔ Click "Text file" ➔ Select data/ev_market_data.csv from this project folder. All 20 columns and data types will map automatically.

2. Pre-Configured Calculated Fields

• Price per km = [Base Price USD] / [Driving Range km]
• Market Share % = SUM([Units Sold Annual]) / TOTAL(SUM([Units Sold Annual]))
• Revenue Billions = [Annual Revenue USD] / 1000000000

3. Publish to Tableau Public

Save workbook to Tableau Public (Server ➔ Tableau Public ➔ Save to Tableau Public As...). Copy the published link to use as your secondary Demo Link.
